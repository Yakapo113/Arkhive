Actual payments by phase: Meta Sweetwater 1

The only deal that has paid anything so far. Meta built it in three phases, each with its own 20-year clock. The 2018 budget modeled it as one.

The deals

Walk-away risk. The posted participation agreements for Meta's Sweetwater 4 and QTS let the company end the deal by notice, don't require it to keep operating, and have no clawback if a building closes. The posted copies are unsigned drafts.

How these numbers are built

  • Projected: each deal's project area budget on the city's RDA page. Sweetwater 1 (May 2018), Sweetwater 2 (amended March 2021), Sweetwater 3 (August 2022) and Triple Tail (July 2024 draft) list amounts year by year, used as printed.
  • Sweetwater 4: the budget's yearly table is posted only as a scanned image, so its 40-year totals (November 2025 budget in the fire district's interlocal agreement) are spread evenly. Real years will start lower and ramp up.
  • Actual: Utah State Tax Commission "Year-End Project Increment Paid by Entity" reports, tax years 2021 to 2025, posted by the Utah County Auditor. They show what was paid to the RDA, not what the entities kept, so "kept" is projected only.
  • After sharing ends: each budget's estimate of the full yearly tax once the deal expires, assuming values hold. If the equipment keeps depreciating, the real amount will be lower.
  • Splitting the RDA share by entity: printed for Sweetwater 1 and 4. For the others it's split in the same proportions as the amounts each entity keeps, since every entity shares at the same rate.
  • Schools: Alpine School District levied through 2025. Lake Mountain School District takes over the Eagle Mountain area in July 2027.
  • Years are tax years. The money reaches the RDA and the entities the following spring. These are estimates, not an official forecast.
