Privacy Policy

Effective July 27, 2026

HomeGuard ("we", "us") is provided by Jacob M. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using HomeGuard you agree to this policy.

Information you provide

  • Account: your email address and password (passwords are stored securely by our authentication provider, never in plain text).
  • Your home: address, and property details such as year built, size, and type.
  • Equipment: the appliances and systems you add — type, brand, model number, install date, your notes, and any photos you take of nameplates or equipment.
  • Service pros (vendor accounts): business name, contact info, trades, hours, and business photos.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic app usage and diagnostics needed to operate the service.
  • A push-notification token (if you enable reminders) so we can send maintenance reminders.

How we use your information

  • To build and maintain your personalized home-maintenance plan.
  • To send the reminders you ask for.
  • To connect you with local service professionals when you choose to contact them.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the app.

Service providers we share data with

We use trusted third parties to run HomeGuard. We share only what each needs:

  • Supabase — secure hosting, database, authentication, and file storage.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — to generate your maintenance plan, we send equipment details (type, brand, model). We do not send your name, address, or account email for this purpose.
  • Google — address lookup and finding local service businesses.
  • YouTube — to suggest relevant how-to videos for tasks.

When you choose to contact a service professional, we share the details needed to complete that request (such as your name, the job, and contact info you provide). We do not sell your personal information.

Data retention & security

  • We keep your information while your account is active.
  • We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit, access controls) to protect your data.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and associated data at any time from Profile → "Delete my account." This removes your home, equipment, tasks, and account record.

Children

HomeGuard is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your home, equipment, and tasks in the app at any time.
  • You can turn reminders on or off in Profile.
  • You can request access to or deletion of your data by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by a new effective date here.
